Final design

Indent

A config block: a parent line, two indented children, the last one trimmed.

  • The shape of every IOS config block, instantly familiar to network engineers
  • The trimmed last line is the 'refine': noise cut away
  • Three pills on a 256 grid; a heavier small-size cut keeps it clear at 16 px
  • Keeps the existing amber-on-navy identity
